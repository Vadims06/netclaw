NetClaw Mobile is officially on the Apple App Store. 📱⌚

Your phone talks directly, securely, to your own self-hosted network agent — no Slack, no
Discord, no Teams, no Webex, nobody's cloud in the middle. Just your phone and your Border.

with Face ID. Send it a photo of a blinking port LED. Check your Border's health from a Home
Screen widget, a Lock Screen widget, or your Apple Watch.
